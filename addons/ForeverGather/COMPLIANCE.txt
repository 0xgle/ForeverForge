FOREVER GATHER — DESIGN / COMPLIANCE NOTES
==========================================

The addon is intentionally local-first and passive:
- no automated movement
- no automated targeting
- no automated casting
- no protected-action execution
- no combat decision engine
- no background network/API calls
- no advertisements or in-game monetization prompts
- no required external executable
- no obfuscated source code

Smart Routes are visual suggestions calculated only from locally known gathering locations.
The player retains full control over movement and every gameplay action.
