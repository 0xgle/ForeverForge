Forever Gatherer — Expedition 3.0
Author: 0xgle

Based on the provided ForeverGather 2.7.0 project; original MIT notice retained.
No external addon libraries bundled. TomTom integration is optional.
Header, emblem, material and profession atlas: generated specifically for this
release using the built-in image generation tool. Runtime TGA conversions and
atlas slices are included in Media. Hollow map markers are original geometric
assets. Fonts are referenced from the WoW client, not redistributed.
